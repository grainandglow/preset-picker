Preset Picker · a free app from Grain & Glow

Give every photo its own look.

I shoot for fun, and I got tired of clicking through presets one photo at a time. So I made a little app for it. Pop in your memory card, pick a preset for each shot, and the photos land in Lightroom with the look already on.

Free · Mac & Windows

How it works

  1. 01 — Choose

    Open your shoot

    Put in your SD card and the app finds it. Your photos are grouped by the day you took them, so last weekend's walk is one click away.

  2. 02 — Pick

    A preset per photo

    Choose a preset under each shot, or select a few and give them the same look. Open any photo big and drag the slider to compare.

  3. 03 — Export

    Straight into Lightroom

    You get a new folder on your Desktop. Import it into Lightroom and your raw photos open with their preset already applied.

For people who shoot for fun.
Raw files
Works with NEF, CR3, ARW, DNG and other raw formats. It reads the camera's own preview, so even big files open fast.
Your presets
Uses the .xmp presets you export from Lightroom, mine or anyone else's.
The preview
The before/after in the app is a close guess. Lightroom always does the real edit from the exact preset settings.
JPEG & HEIC
Lightroom only picks up the preset automatically for raw photos. For phone pictures, apply it inside Lightroom.

Installing on a Mac · 4 steps

  1. Open the download in your Downloads folder; it unzips by itself. Drag Preset Picker onto Applications in the sidebar.

  2. Open Preset Picker. The first time, macOS stops it because the app isn't notarised by Apple yet. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll all the way down to Security and click Open Anyway next to "Preset Picker was blocked", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

  4. That's it: Preset Picker opens, and from now on it starts normally. Click Allow when it asks to see your memory card or Desktop.

Installing on Windows · 6 steps

  1. Open PresetPicker-Setup-x64.exe from your Downloads. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. That's only because the app isn't code-signed yet.

  2. Choose Install for me only. That way you don't need an admin password.

  3. Keep the suggested folder and click Next.

  4. Tick Create a desktop shortcut if you'd like one, then click Next.

  5. Click Install. It takes a few seconds.

  6. Leave Launch Preset Picker ticked and click Finish. From now on you'll find it in the Start menu.

On an ARM PC it's the same installer; Windows 11 runs it for you. To remove the app, go to Settings → Apps.

Why does it ask for folders?
App Store apps live in Apple's sandbox. They can only open what you point them to. Pick your presets folder, your card and where to save once, and it remembers.
Is my stuff uploaded anywhere?
No. Your photos and presets stay on your Mac. The app doesn't send anything anywhere.
